# Stage 1c — a richly differentiated panel beats one solo call

**2026-09-09.** Store `results-study1c`. Registration: `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md` Appendix L (design) and Appendix M (the ceiling defect, the re-collect rule, and the result).

## Headline

Stage 1b found that a **thin-stance** 5-agent panel was *significantly worse* than a single solo call at matched pool size (−2.1338, Holm p 0.0007). Stage 1c replaces the one-sentence stances with five richly differentiated personas — weirdo, philosopher, MBA, poet, product manager — varying stance text, strategy and effort together.

**The sign reverses.**

| S1c-2 | `S1C-RICH − S1C-SOLO6X10` | **+30.2126** | 0.6931 | [28.854, 31.571] | <0.0001 |

## The three things worth taking away

**1. Stance richness is what moved it.** The SOLO60 bridge check (Appendix L item 4) passes: this run's solo arm reproduces Stage 1b's at 44.369 vs 44.570 (−0.201), with matching rarefaction floors of 50. Both registered conditions are met, so the cross-run comparison is reportable as clearly-labelled secondary: **rich panel 46.025 vs thin panel 42.495**. Same geometry, same model, same pool size — different stance text.

**2. Undifferentiated parallelism is nearly worthless.** `S1C-SOLO6X10` — ten blind, identical agents, one round, no sharing — produces a full 60-idea pool that clusters to about **15 distinct ideas**. A 75% collapse, verified on a raw stored cell rather than inferred from a mean. Ten undifferentiated agents are worth less than a third of *one* solo call asked for 60 ideas. This is the cleanest single result in the study, and it is the direct answer to "are the agents just producing the same average ideas and getting deduplicated out?" — yes, when nothing differentiates them.

**3. The economics are thin even so.** A **14.4×** cost increase buys **+3.8%** rarefied distinct ideas. The panel wins, but it does not win in a way that pays for itself on this metric.

## What this cannot say

- **Nothing about personas specifically.** Three axes — stance, strategy, effort — are bundled by registration. `+1.6728` is the bundle.
- **Nothing about quality.** `distinct_k` is distinctness. **Issue #16's judge gate has never been run**, and it is now the binding constraint on every claim in this study. `weirdo` is explicitly prompted for strangeness and is the one slot that could buy distinctness with incoherence.
- **The effect is below the design's own MDE** (registered 1.89; observed 1.67), and Holm applied no correction to S1c-1 — it is the larger p, so 0.0158 is raw. Replicate before leaning on it.

## How the run got here

The first attempt lost **139 of 144** `S1C-RICH` cells. Every failure was `parse_failure` / `cause=partial_truncated` with all five agents replying and a healthy pool (median 48 candidates) discarded by `classifyUndersizedPool` (#102) — one truncated reply of ten discards the cell, so 18.5% per reply became 96.5% per cell.

The cause was **not** a missing effort model in the sizer — that was the first diagnosis and it was wrong. `maxTokensForIdeas` is effort-keyed. The defect was that #160's per-request floor, which correctly identified a *fixed* per-reply cost, was calibrated by a probe run entirely at effort `high` — and that fixed cost is itself a function of effort. A 30-reply uncensored probe found max-effort replies running to 18,209 tokens against an 11,640 ceiling, 6 of 12 over. The floor is now keyed: `{max: 45523}`. `low` and `high` measured clean and were deliberately left alone.

`classifyUndersizedPool` was **not** loosened. Editing an inclusion rule after finding it killed the treatment arm is selecting on the outcome.

The 288 control cells were **reused, not re-collected**: both control arms run at `high`, so their requests are byte-identical across the fix (26,250 and 6,873), asserted against hard-coded pre-fix literals. The 5 surviving old-hash `S1C-RICH` cells are **excluded** from every contrast — they are exactly the cells where none of the four max-effort replies truncated, the most outcome-selected subset in the store. No `distinct_k` was inspected before that rule was registered.
