## Headline: only pool size moves `distinct_k`

Mixed model, brief as a random effect, contrasts against the centre point (N=30, `effort: high`, stance neutral, `strategy: cot`):

| Condition | Effect on `distinct_k` | 95% CI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `effort: low` | +0.04 | [−1.51, 1.59] |
| `effort: max` | +0.88 | [−0.68, 2.43] |
| stance PRAGMATIST | −1.54 | [−3.09, 0.01] |
| stance CONTRARIAN | 0.00 | [−1.55, 1.55] |
| `strategy: direct` | −0.58 | [−2.14, 0.97] |
| **N = 10** | **−17.71** | [−19.93, −15.50] |
| **N = 60** | **+22.58** | [+20.38, +24.79] |

Achieved MDE is ≈ 2.2 distinct ideas, so effort, stance and chain-of-thought each move the metric by less than ~8% of a 27-idea pool. `effort: max` costs roughly **4.5× the output tokens** of `high` for +0.88 with a CI spanning zero.

## N buys pool size, not distinctness

Rarefied per contrast (Appendix C item 4), matched to the smaller pool:

| Contrast | rarefied N | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| centre vs N=60 | 30 | 26.58 ± 2.73 vs **26.45 ± 2.02** |
| N=10 vs centre | 10 | 9.09 ± 1.53 vs 9.50 ± 0.51 |

At a matched pool of 30 the N=30 and N=60 conditions are indistinguishable. The entire raw gap (26.79 → 49.38) is pool size: per-idea distinctness is flat in N, with no diminishing returns up to 60. Raw `distinct_k` by condition — required alongside the rarefied value by Appendix C item 5 — is in the CSV.

**What this establishes is a denominator, not a comparison.** Every Stage 1a condition was a solo call; no panel was run. A single solo call at N=60 yields ≈ 49.4 distinct ideas, and panel arms produce ~60-idea pools (5 agents × 6 ideas × 2 rounds), so a panel must clear ≈ 49.4 at equal pool size to have earned its complexity. Whether it does is untested here.

## σ²_ba was overestimated by ~64×

Appendix E item 7 registered `brief:arm = 77.85` as provisional, transcribed from `#129` and flagged as "the least stable number in the study" — estimated from **two** briefs — and explicitly bound the B = 48 / R = 2 replication table to Stage 1a's re-estimate. That re-estimate, on 12 briefs:

| Component | Registered (2-brief smoke) | Re-estimated, matched N=30 | Re-estimated, pooled across N |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `brief:arm` | **77.85** | **1.21** | 2.49 |
| `brief` | 21.99 | 4.98 | 5.54 |

Achieved SE per arm contrast at 12 briefs × 2 replicates is **0.79**, giving MDE ≈ 2.2 where §3.4's registered table predicted **10.3** for that same design — the study is ~4.7× more sensitive than registered.

A mechanism for the overestimate is identifiable and testable: fitting across mixed pool sizes inflates the interaction term (the pooled-across-N fit gives 2.49 against 1.21 at matched N), and the smoke pooled arm A at ~30 ideas with arm B at ~60. **B = 48 / R = 2 is therefore heavily over-powered.** Stage 1b and the main grid should be re-sized from these components before further spend; that re-derivation is not done here.

## The one failure is diagnostic

`arm=S1-N10 | brief=prod-07 | rep=1` — `parse_failure`, cause `unparseable_complete`. The model returned a **single** JSON object containing all ten ideas as a numbered list, with a raw control character inside the string, and stopped cleanly on `end_turn` at 1,304 tokens. Not truncation, so the raised `max_tokens` cap (Appendix F item 6) is irrelevant to it; `salvageCandidateArray` recovered nothing because there was only ever one malformed object to recover. Format collapse at the smallest N, stored as terminal data about the arm. Accounting reconciled 191 + 1 = 192.

`S1-EMAX` completed all 24 cells with zero failures — the condition that would have truncated systematically under the pre-`#130` 4,650-token cap.

## Store handling

The raw results **are** committed, at [`data/study1-stage1a/cells.jsonl`](../data/study1-stage1a/cells.jsonl) — all 956 records, 2.9 MB.

The on-disk store was 130 MB, but **97.9% of that was Voyage embedding vectors** (124.3 MB of 127.0 MB; idea text is 1.7 MB, other metadata 1.0 MB). `result.pool` is therefore the one field removed. It is derived rather than primary — a deterministic function of the candidate text under the pinned embedder and registered threshold — and re-embedding the entire run costs **$0.0034**, the `excluded (non-provider)` line in the spend report above. Every embedding-derived metric (`distinct_k`, `poolDiversity`, `collapseRate`) is additionally stored per cell, so the reported numbers need no recomputation.

`.gitignore` covers `results/` with the stated reason "Never commit raw provider replies (they can be large and may echo brief text under NDA)", but sibling stores created by `--results-dir` (Appendix D item 3) were never added to that rule and `results-study1/` slipped past it; the pattern is now extended. The NDA half of that warning does not bind here — every brief in the corpus is already public in this repository, and `prod-07` is a verbatim published prompt. The size half did bind, and stripping the vectors is the response.

`results-pilot/` remains tracked as a store (Appendix E item 8); it is 59 records. Nothing about it changes here.

## What is not answered

- **Whether panels beat a solo call.** No panel ran. Arm A′ (persona-disabled panel), which isolates "personas" from "sampling five times," is also unrun.
- **Whether stance differentiates *between* agents.** Stage 1a showed a stance barely changes what one agent produces; a panel's claim is that five *different* stances spread five agents apart. The former weakens the prior for the latter without testing it.
- **A caveat for any future panel result from this harness:** `DEFAULT_PERSONAS` varies temperature across personas (0.4 / 0.9 / 0.6 / 1.0 / 1.0), but §3.3 strips sampling parameters universally and both request builders are allowlists that never send `temperature`. Shipped `ideate-core` differentiates personas by stance **and** strategy **and** temperature; this harness sends stance and strategy only. Panel arms here are less differentiated than the product they measure, and that must be stated wherever a panel result is reported.
